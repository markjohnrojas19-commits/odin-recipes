Graham Cracker Crust

Description

"A graham cracker crust is a simple dessert base made from crushed graham crackers, sugar, and butter. It is easy to prepare and pairs well with many pie and cheesecake fillings"

Ingredients:

  • 1 ½ cups finely ground graham cracker crumbs
  • ⅓ cup white sugar
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon (Optional)

Steps:

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Combine graham cracker crumbs, sugar, melted butter, and cinnamon in a mixing bowl; mix until well blended.
  3. Press mixture into the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie plate.
  4. Follow directions for a baked or no-bake crust.
